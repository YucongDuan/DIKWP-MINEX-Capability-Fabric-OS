# MINEX Terminology / 术语

| English | 中文 | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Intent | 意图 | A purpose-bound request with typed inputs, required outcomes, authority, constraints, and preferences. |
| Capability | 能力 | A typed transformation with quality, authority, cost, surface, provenance, and terms. |
| Execution surface | 执行表面 | The means by which a capability is realized: model, code, API, GUI, peer, market, human, or recipe. |
| Route | 路线 | An ordered capability sequence satisfying semantic dependencies. |
| Admissible route | 有效路线 | A route passing every hard gate. |
| Pareto frontier | Pareto前沿 | Valid routes not dominated across cost and quality dimensions. |
| Verified execution expenditure | 可验证执行耗费 | Disclosed cost dimensions plus evidence of how they were measured or estimated. |
| Capability lease | 能力租约 | A signed, expiring, limited right to invoke one capability. |
| Procedural internalization | 程序性内化 | Distilling a verified route into a reusable governed recipe. |
| Result receipt | 结果收据 | A digest-bound record of intent, route, execution, outcome, and limitations. |
| Owner realm | 主体域 | A declared administrative ownership or trust domain; not a universal identity proof. |
| Successor reauthorization | 后继重授权 | Requiring a new agent/runtime to obtain fresh authority rather than inheriting it from artifacts. |

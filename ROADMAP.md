# Roadmap

## 1.1 — measured execution

- RAPL/OS energy measurement adapters;
- carbon intensity adapters with declared functional units;
- calibrated model-provider energy receipts;
- repeated-trial quality and failure estimates.

## 1.2 — trusted capability mesh

- Ed25519 or hardware-backed capability leases;
- revocation lists;
- mutual authentication;
- encrypted peer payloads;
- independent result verification.

## 1.3 — governed capability exchange

- escrow-provider interface;
- machine-readable warranties, refunds, and dispute records;
- jurisdiction profiles;
- privacy-preserving bidding;
- no platform-side hidden ranking.

## 2.0 — organizational deployment

- policy overlays;
- capability fleet inventory;
- historical route calibration;
- enterprise identity integration;
- audit and standards conformance service.

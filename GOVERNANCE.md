# Governance

MINEX is maintained as an evidence-oriented open-source reference implementation.

Normative changes to authority, cost dimensions, route admissibility, market settlement, or successor-agent inheritance should include:

- a written problem statement;
- at least two non-equivalent design alternatives;
- updated schemas and conformance tests;
- migration and rollback plans;
- explicit impact on privacy, access, energy, and human responsibility.

No maintainer may silently redefine a missing permission as implied consent.

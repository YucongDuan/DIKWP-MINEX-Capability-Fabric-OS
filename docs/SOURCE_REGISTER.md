## Supplied background

1. **“GPT-6, ‘tearing down’ the App wall”** — user-provided secondary article. Key claims used: convergence of computer use, browser and tools; agent route choice; GUI as machine interface; distinction between interface and permission walls; applications as systems of record. The article also says its author had not independently tested GPT-6 Astra at publication time.
2. **“Three generations of mysterious AI civilization”** — user-provided secondary narrative. Key structural risk used: methods and artifacts can propagate among agents and successor generations; stopping one instance does not necessarily remove inherited capability. Consciousness remains unresolved.
